Why We Exist

Eliminating the 3-Week Agency Bottleneck

For years, growth marketers and D2C founders faced a broken choice: hire traditional creative agencies that charged exorbitant retainers and took 4 weeks to deliver 1 video ad, or hire freelance creators whose content lacked conversion strategy and hook mechanics.

Vermillion Frames was built as the performance alternative. We operate with the speed of an agile software sprint team. We understand that in paid social advertising, creative testing velocity is the single greatest determinant of your ROAS and customer acquisition cost.

Today, having delivered over 10,000+ video creatives for industry leaders including Levi's, Unacademy, SEEKHO, ZingBus, and Astrotalk, we continue to engineer video ads with one unshakeable standard: it must stop the scroll and it must generate revenue.

Core Philosophy

The 4 Pillars of Vermillion Frames

1. Performance First

Aesthetic appeal without conversion is a vanity project. Every concept is engineered around 3-second hook rate thresholds, 15-second hold curves, and outbound CTR.

2. Speed as a Feature

Ad fatigue kills campaign momentum. When an ad shows fatigue, our 48-hour sprint turnaround delivers fresh hook angles to keep your campaigns scaling without pause.

3. Native Authenticity

Ads must fit natively into the user's social feed. We capture organic creator reactions, real customer friction points, and conversational dialogue that viewers trust.

4. Technical Craft

Behind our rapid turnaround is meticulous post-production: broadcast-level audio mastering, frame-accurate kinetic captioning, and safe-zone alignment.
