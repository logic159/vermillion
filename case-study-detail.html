The Challenge: Creative Fatigue & Sizing Drop-Offs

Levi's D2C growth team was experiencing severe creative fatigue with high-gloss studio lookbook assets. While the brand assets looked aesthetically polished, their Meta 3-second hook rate was hovering at 18%, causing CPMs to spike and cold audience ROAS to stall at 2.1x.

Customer post-purchase data indicated the single largest barrier to online denim purchases was sizing uncertainty ("Will this waist fit my hips without gapping?"). Studio models failed to answer this question.

The Creative Hypothesis: Relatable Body Diversity & Pattern Interrupts

Vermillion Frames deployed a 12-variation UGC testing sprint. Instead of shooting 1 standard ad, we cast 3 creators across diverse body types and filmed a modular matrix:

The Deployed 12-Variation Sprint Matrix:

  • Hook A (Visual Pattern Interrupt): Creator physically measuring the waist gap on camera with the line: "If your jeans gap at the back, watch this."
  • Hook B (The Skeptical Try-On): Creator holding up the pants with skepticism: "I ordered 4 sizes thinking none would fit..."
  • Hook C (Direct Problem Callout): Fast cut of 3 women struggling with stiff competitor denim vs Levi's stretch.
  • Body Angles: 2 distinct flows focusing on (1) all-day comfort stretch vs (2) styling versatility for work and night outs.
  • CTAs: Free exchanges / fit guarantee vs limited-time 20% first-order discount.

The Results: Scaling Winning Angles

Within 72 hours of launching the test campaign, Hook 3B emerged as the clear statistical winner with a 44.2% 3-second hook rate (more than double their historical 18% baseline) and an outbound CTR of 3.4%.
