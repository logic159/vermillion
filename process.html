The Performance Framework

The 5-Step Creative Engine Built For Velocity

How we take growth brands from creative audit to live winning video ads without endless meetings or production bottlenecks.

01
Discovery & Audit

Ad Account & Customer Objection Mining

We start by reviewing your historical ad account performance, top-spending creatives, drop-off timecodes, customer reviews, and competitor ads. We identify exactly why past ads succeeded or failed and pinpoint high-converting angles.

Deliverable: Creative Audit Sheet + Angle Opportunities Matrix within 48 hours of onboarding.
02
Concepting & Copy

The 3-Second Hook Scripting Matrix

Our direct-response copywriters craft modular scripts. Each concept includes 3 visual and verbal hook openers (pattern interrupts, problem calls, curiosity triggers), 2 value-prop body angles, and 2 distinct CTA closes.

Deliverable: Complete multi-hook script deck with visual director notes for client sign-off.
03
Shoot & Capture

Creator Casting, AI Generation & Studio Filming

Depending on the sprint format, we dispatch product to vetted native creators, generate photorealistic AI avatar streams, or film in our commercial studio with cinema camera gear. All assets are filmed in native 9:16 vertical 4K.

Deliverable: Raw B-roll footage, multiple creator takes, and high-fidelity audio assets.
04
Editing & Formatting

High-Retention Motion Post-Production

Our editors apply kinetic text typography, sound effects, trending audio cues, and platform safe-zone positioning. We export all 12 ad variations per concept in Meta and TikTok compliant formats.

Deliverable: Google Drive / Frame.io batch ready for immediate media buyer campaign upload.
05
Analysis & Scale

Testing Data Review & 48-Hour Iteration Sprints

After 7 days of live media spend, we analyze the 3-second hook rates, 15-second hold rates, and ROAS. We isolate the winning angles and produce new hook iterations and cutdowns in under 48 hours to prevent creative fatigue.

Deliverable: Weekly Creative Performance Report + Instant Iteration Cutdowns.
